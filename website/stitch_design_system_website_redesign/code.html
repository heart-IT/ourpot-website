Archival Integrity

Your household ledger lives with you.

Your records stay strictly on your physical devices. OurPot uses peer-to-peer encrypted CRDT synchronization between household members. We operate zero central databases storing what you purchase, who paid for groceries, or your home settlement balances.

The Sync Mechanism

Encrypted Peer-to-Peer Protocol

Encrypted P2P Sync

Zero central server in the middle. Devices exchange encrypted Datapack deltas directly over Wi-Fi, Bluetooth mesh, or end-to-end relays.

1

Create Household

Initializes a private encrypted vault on your local device. This becomes your device's sovereign source of truth.

2

Invite Household

Invite members using an encrypted QR code or secure peer link. Devices exchange signed cryptographic keys directly.

3

Direct Peer Sync

Devices sync records directly using end-to-end encryption. Built on the Datapack mesh protocol, requiring zero third-party oversight.

What happens if...

Read our Architecture & Security Deep-dive arrow_forward

What if I lose my phone?

phone_iphone

Your ledger is distributed. Simply install the app on a new device and accept an invite from any other active household member to sync the full history back seamlessly. You can also import from your weekly local backups.

Does it work without internet?

wifi_off

Yes. All recording, editing, and balancing functions work 100% offline. Devices will automatically synchronize in the background as soon as connectivity or local Wi-Fi mesh is restored.

How do I move my data?

file_download

You hold the keys. Export your complete ledger history to standard CSV or JSON formats at any time directly from the application settings. No approval required, no fees, zero lock-in.

How is my data secured?

lock

End-to-end AES-256 and XChaCha20 encryption ensures that even if someone intercepted your sync data over airwaves, they couldn't read your household purchases. Only your paired devices hold the decryption keys.

What is a household?

A household is any group that repeatedly shares real-life costs. OurPot is tailored for any circle that needs a shared, calm record of living.
